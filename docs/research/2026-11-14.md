## 1. Dependency advisories since 2026-11-07
None that affect it. A search of [rustsec.org](https://rustsec.org/advisories/) for tokio, clap, anyhow, thiserror and libc found nothing new. The November entries are in crates it does not use: [RUSTSEC-2026-0440](https://rustsec.org/advisories/) (astral-tokio-tar unmaintained, info), calamine and ant-releases. The older tar PAX advisories ([RUSTSEC-2026-0068](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0068), [-0145](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0145)) hit crates it does not use, since fio-xfs has its own PAX writer. They do show that PAX size-header handling is a known trap, and `tests/` could check that our writer's PAX records round-trip through GNU tar and bsdtar.

## 2. Specs
- No on-disk XFS format change found. Linux 7.0 added [autonomous self-healing](https://phoronix.com/news/xfs-linux-7.0) and faster parent-pointer operations. Neither changes the format.
- A Linux 7.2-cycle log-recovery fix stops a corrupt dquot buffer error from being discarded. A March 2026 change fixes undersized `l_iclog_roundoff`. Both are in kernel replay. Neither changes how the log is read, but both bear on #15 (replay) and #16 if the log code is ported again.
- Parent pointers are a v5 incompat feature, and mkfs.xfs 7 turns them on by default ([Oracle's write-up](https://blogs.oracle.com/linux/xfs-directory-parent-pointers-in-uek8)). That is #20, and nothing new for it.
