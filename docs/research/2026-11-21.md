Entries from 11-06 to 11-12 are in other crates: actix-http (RUSTSEC-2026-0448, -0455), rmcp (-0461, -0462), hickory-* (-0490, -0491, -0516 to -0522), sqlcipher-src (-0502); see the [Corgea roundup](https://corgea.com/research/rustsec-2026-0455-0454-0437-cve-2026-123272-113663-61536-actix-surrealdb-keyvault-mcp-go-ag2-banks-november-2026). One older tokio advisory: RUSTSEC-2026-0384 (October 2026, a `time::Interval` race on the multi-thread runtime, tokio 1.54.0 yanked, fixed in 1.54.1). It predates the window, and since `tokio = "1"` has no lockfile here, consumers get 1.54.1 or later on a fresh resolve; the `cli` feature enables `rt-multi-thread`, so a consumer pinned to 1.54.0 would be hit only if it was not yanked out of their lock.
